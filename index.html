Research Focus

Core research areas

Research themes may be customized according to current projects, laboratory activities, grants, and publication areas.

01

Abiotic Stress Tolerance

Investigating physiological and molecular mechanisms that enable plants to tolerate drought, salinity, heat, oxidative stress, and combined stress conditions.

02

Plant Developmental Plasticity

Exploring how root and shoot developmental programs are regulated in response to changing environmental conditions and stress signals.

03

Epigenetic Regulation

Examining plant epigenetic memory, chromatin regulation, and heritable stress-responsive mechanisms under abiotic stress.

04

Molecular and Genetic Mechanisms

Applying molecular, genetic, and genomic approaches to identify key pathways, genes, and regulatory networks associated with stress adaptation.

05

Climate-Resilient Agriculture

Translating plant stress research into strategies that support sustainable crop production and resilience under climate change.

06

Scientific Collaboration

Building multidisciplinary and international research partnerships across plant biology, agriculture, biotechnology, and environmental science.
