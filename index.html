About Me

Advancing plant resilience through integrated systems biology.

I am Dr. Md. Mezanur Rahman, a plant scientist and Postdoctoral Research Associate in the Plant Systems Biology Group at Oak Ridge National Laboratory, Oak Ridge, Tennessee, USA.

My research combines plant physiology, high-throughput phenotyping, molecular biology, photosynthesis, plant–microbe interactions, and multi-omics approaches to understand plant adaptation to complex environmental conditions.

My research interests include drought, heat, salinity, waterlogging, nutrient deficiency, heavy metals, combined abiotic stress, photosynthetic resilience, stress signaling, sustainable crop production, and climate-smart agriculture.

Current Research Activity

High-throughput phenotyping and combined stress biology

My current research focuses on developing robust research workflows to understand plant performance, growth dynamics, and stress resilience under complex and changing environmental conditions.

Current Research

High-Throughput Phenotyping

Developing and applying high-throughput phenotyping approaches to quantify plant growth, morphology, architecture, physiological performance, and stress-response traits across controlled and dynamic environments.

Method Development

Method Optimization

Optimizing experimental workflows, phenotyping protocols, imaging pipelines, trait extraction, sampling strategies, data-quality procedures, and analytical methods for reproducible plant systems biology research.

Stress Biology

Combined Abiotic Stress Experiments

Investigating plant responses to combined environmental stresses, including drought, heat, salinity, nutrient limitation, waterlogging, and stress interactions affecting crop productivity and resilience.
