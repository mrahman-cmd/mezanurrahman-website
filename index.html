About Me

Advancing plant resilience for sustainable agriculture.

I am Dr. Md. Mezanur Rahman, a plant scientist and Postdoctoral Research Associate in the Plant Systems Biology Group at Oak Ridge National Laboratory (ORNL), Oak Ridge, Tennessee, USA.

My research integrates plant physiology, biochemistry, molecular biology, photosynthesis, plant–microbe interactions, and multi-omics approaches to understand plant responses to drought, heat, salinity, waterlogging, nutrient deficiency, heavy metals, and combined abiotic stresses.

With approximately 10 years of research experience, I have worked on climate-smart crops, soybean stress tolerance, signaling molecules, phytohormones, agroforestry, tree–crop interactions, and sustainable agricultural systems.

Research Expertise

Research areas and scientific approaches

My work connects fundamental plant biology with practical strategies for improving crop productivity, stress resilience, and sustainability.

01

Abiotic Stress Tolerance

Drought, heat, salinity, nutrient deficiency, waterlogging, submergence, heavy metal stress, and combined stress responses in major crops and tree species.

02

Plant–Microbe Interactions

Beneficial microbial functions, including Bacillus species and Rhizobium japonicum, in root architecture, nodulation, yield, and plant stress adaptation.

03

Photosynthesis Research

Gas exchange, chlorophyll fluorescence, electron transport, carbon assimilation, oxidative stress, and photosynthetic resilience under field and controlled environments.

04

Signaling Molecules & Hormones

Functional roles of acetic acid, ethanol, melatonin, strigolactones, karrikins, salicylic acid, jasmonic acid, silicon, glutathione, and nanoparticles.

05

Omics & Molecular Biology

Transcriptomics, metabolomics, hormonomics, qRT-PCR, DNA/RNA extraction, and molecular investigations of stress-response mechanisms.

06

Agroforestry & Tree–Crop Systems

Tree–crop interactions involving jackfruit, jujube, guava, citrus, eucalyptus, acacia, and high-value crops in climate-sensitive agricultural landscapes.
